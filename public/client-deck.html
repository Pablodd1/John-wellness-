The Core · It Learns

The site learns each client,
like Google and Meta do — transparently.

What it observes Every page viewed · every product opened · every click and like · goals chosen · what goes into the cart · questions asked to the voice assistant.
How it adapts Products re-rank live with a visible match score ("92% — fits your athlete profile") · curation shifts with detected intent · The Cart predicts refills · the assistant answers from their own history.
Unlike the ad giants Every score explains itself — "Why 92%?" shows the exact reasons. No black box.
Privacy-native by design Learning runs only while the client consents — one switch off and it stops. Nothing sold, nothing shared across sites.

The result: the longer they use it, the more the platform feels like it was built for them — because it was, by their own behavior.
